Trade questions

Can I get samples first?
Yes. We post samples of both grades free of charge, usually the next working day. Tell us which grades and we will send them with the certificate of analysis.
What are your payment terms?
Bank transfer in cleared funds before despatch, or cash on collection. On repeat orders we can discuss terms once a trading history is established.
Can I collect rather than pay delivery?
Yes, and it is usually cheaper. The stock is held in London with forklift access. Give us a day and we will have the pallets ready.
Do you supply unbranded for own label?
Yes, from 500kg upward. We ship plain and you apply your own artwork, or we can quote for applying labels you supply.
What documentation comes with it?
The salt was imported as edible grade under a Pakistan Department of Plant Protection phytosanitary certificate and an OICCI certificate of origin. Copies of those, the packing list and the bill of lading go out with samples as standard. A laboratory certificate of analysis can be obtained from the mine on request.
How much can you supply?
Multi-tonne quantities are available immediately from UK stock. For requirements beyond current holding we can arrange a further import, though that carries the normal lead time.
